\section*{Preface}
\markboth{Preface}{}
A fair warning before we start. The topics here are not the gentlest ones in competitive programming, and most of them expect you to be comfortable with the basics already, like recursion, sorting, and simple graph searches. Some pages will need a second reading, or a third. That is perfectly fine. Give a topic enough time and focus, and sooner or later it will click.

Nobody walks in knowing everything, and neither did we. Every page here was written by someone who once stared at the same code and wondered why it worked. Hopefully it saves you some of that staring.
