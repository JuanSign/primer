\topic{Troubleshooting}
{\small
\textbf{Before submitting.} Max-size test for time and memory, overflow, the right file, debug output removed.
\textbf{Wrong answer.} Reread the statement. Clear globals between test cases. Edge cases: $n = 0, 1$, all equal, sorted, negative. Overflow in intermediate products, \code|1 << k| on \code|ll|, negative \code|%|, rounding of negatives. Precision: compare with eps, avoid \code|==| on doubles. Off-by-one in loops and binary search. Multiple answers allowed? Uninitialized variables. Shadowed names, \code|i| for \code|j|.
\textbf{Runtime error.} Out of bounds (try \code|-D_GLIBCXX_DEBUG|), recursion too deep (iterate, or the stack flag on Windows), division by zero, iterator invalidated by \code|push_back| or \code|erase|, \code|pop| on empty.
\textbf{Time limit.} Infinite loop, complexity at max $n$, copying vectors by value, \code|endl|, \code|map| where an array works, hacked \code|unordered_map| (use SplitMix), too many \code|new|.
\textbf{Memory.} \code|4n| segment trees, \code|vector<vector>| overhead, recursion.
\par}
