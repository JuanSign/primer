\topic{Flow and Matching Facts}
{\small
\textbf{K\"onig.} Bipartite: max matching = min vertex cover, max independent set = $V -$ matching. Cover: walk alternating paths from free left vertices, take unvisited left and visited right.
\textbf{DAG path cover} = $n -$ matching on $v_{\text{out}} \to u_{\text{in}}$. With reachability edges it is the min chain cover = max antichain (Dilworth).
\textbf{Lower bounds} $[lo, hi]$ on $a \to b$: cap $hi - lo$, $ex_b \mathrel{+}= lo$, $ex_a \mathrel{-}= lo$. Add $S' \to v$ (cap $ex_v > 0$), $v \to T'$ ($-ex_v$) and $t \to s$ ($\infty$). Feasible iff the $S'T'$ flow saturates. Then remove $t \to s$ and push $s \to t$ for max flow.
\textbf{Closure.} Profit $p_i$: $s \to i$ cap $p_i$ if $p_i > 0$, else $i \to t$ cap $-p_i$. ``$i$ needs $j$'': $i \to j$ cap $\infty$. Best $= \sum p_i^+ -$ min cut.
\textbf{Disjoint paths.} Edge: unit caps. Vertex: split $v$ into $v_{in} \to v_{out}$ cap 1.
\textbf{Hall.} Left side saturable iff $|N(S)| \ge |S|$ for all $S$.
\par}

\topic{Tree and Graph Techniques}
{\small
\textbf{Euler tour.} Subtree of $v$ = positions $[tin_v, tout_v)$, so subtree queries become range queries. Path queries: $+x$ at $tin$, $-x$ at $tout$, then root-to-$v$ sums.
\textbf{Small to large.} Merge the smaller set into the larger one (keep the heavy child's set), $O(n \log n)$ moves.
\textbf{Rerooting.} Compute $down_v$ from children, then $up_u$ from $up_v$ and siblings with prefix and suffix merges, so every vertex gets the answer as root.
\textbf{Functional graph.} Binary lifting \code|up[j][v]| gives the $k$-th successor; every component is one cycle with trees hanging on it.
\textbf{Degree sequences.} Erd\H{o}s--Gallai: $d_1 \ge \dots \ge d_n$ with even sum is graphic iff $\sum_{i \le k} d_i \le k(k-1) + \sum_{i > k}\min(d_i, k)$ for all $k$.
\textbf{MST.} Kruskal with a DSU. The minimum bottleneck path lies on the MST. Second best MST: add one edge, drop the max on its cycle (binary lifting).
\textbf{Bipartite.} Iff no odd cycle. Max weight closure, vertex cover and path cover: see the flow facts.
\par}
